Support

In effect from 3 November 2026

Email theingredientlist.co@gmail.com and include your phone model and Android version. Replies usually come within two working days.

The edges were not detected

Put the document on a surface that contrasts with it and make sure all four corners are in frame. If it still misses, drag the four corner handles yourself, or tap Find edges to try again. The app hands you the handles rather than guessing when it is not confident.

The scan came out crooked or squashed

Corners placed slightly inside the page will skew the result. Use the magnifier that appears while you drag to land each corner exactly on the paper corner.

The text is patchy in Paper mode

Paper mode converts to pure black and white, which struggles with faint pencil or coloured highlighting. Switch to Grey or Colour for those.

I cannot find my scans in my gallery

Scans stay inside the app rather than the camera roll, which is deliberate. To get one out, open the document and use Share or Save PDF.

I forgot my PIN

There is no recovery and no backdoor, which is the point of it. Clearing the app’s data in Android Settings removes the lock, and removes every saved document with it.

Will my scans sync to another phone?

No. There is no cloud and no sync. Export a PDF and move it yourself.

Is there a subscription?

No. The app is free and unlimited.
