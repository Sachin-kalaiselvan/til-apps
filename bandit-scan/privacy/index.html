Bandit Scan

Privacy Policy

In effect from 3 November 2026

Bandit Scan collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing outside your phone. There is no account, no server and no analytics. This page sets that out in detail.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no profile and no identifier for you or your device. We never see your documents, because there is nowhere for them to go.

Where your scans live

Photographs you scan are processed on the phone itself and saved in the app’s own storage on that phone. They are not uploaded, backed up or synced. If you uninstall the app, or use Delete everything in Settings, they are gone and cannot be recovered by us or anyone else.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access only so you can photograph a document, and reads a picture from your gallery only when you choose one. Images are used for the scan you asked for and nothing else.

Sharing a PDF

When you tap Share, Android hands the file to the app you pick. From that point the file is governed by that app’s privacy policy, not this one. We are not involved in the transfer and keep no copy.

Your PIN

If you set an app lock, the PIN itself is never written down. Only a one-way cryptographic hash of it is stored on your phone. It cannot be read back or recovered, including by us. The PIN stops someone opening the app; it does not encrypt the saved pages, so a person with full access to your unlocked phone could still reach them by other means.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. Fonts and the PDF writer are built into it, so it works with no connection at all.

Advertising and tracking

There are no third-party advertising or analytics libraries in the app, and no tracking identifiers are collected or shared.

Children

Bandit Scan is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version appears here with a new date, and material changes are noted in the release notes on Google Play.
