Bolt Up

Unscrew, sort and stack the nuts until every bolt holds one colour.

Each bolt carries a stack of coloured nuts. Lift the top nut and drop it onto a bolt with room — and a matching colour on top. Sort every bolt down to a single colour to clear the level.

No timer. Works offline. Free.
