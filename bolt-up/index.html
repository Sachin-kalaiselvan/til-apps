Bolt Up

Unscrew bolts into the holder of the matching colour and clear every plate.

Each plate is held down by coloured bolts. Unscrew one and it drops into the holder of the same colour. Clear every bolt from every plate to finish the level — but holders fill up, so pick your order.

Chapters

  • Starter benchThe base game. Match bolts to holders and clear the plates.
  • Rust beltRusted bolts take two turns to come out.
  • Short benchOnly two holders open. The third is padlocked until you've cleared six bolts.
  • Full shiftRust, two holders and seven plates, all at once.

Four chapters of 30 levels each.

Daily streak. Coins. Themes you unlock as you play.

Ads are optional: watch a short video for a hint or an undo if you want one. Between-level ads are capped, and never appear in the middle of a level.
