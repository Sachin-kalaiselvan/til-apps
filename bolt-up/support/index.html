Bolt Up

Something broken, or an idea for the game? Email theingredientlist.co@gmail.com and include your device model and Android version — it makes bugs far easier to track down.

Common questions

A bolt won't come out in one go.
That's a rusted bolt. It takes two turns to unscrew — you'll see it in Rust belt and Full shift.

One holder is locked.
In Short bench the third holder is padlocked until you've cleared six bolts. Plan around having only two.

The ad didn't give me my hint or undo.
The reward only lands if the video plays all the way through. If you closed it early, nothing is granted. If it completed and you still got nothing, email us.

Will an ad interrupt a level?
No. Videos only play when you ask for a hint or an undo. Between-level ads are capped and never appear mid-level.

I lost my progress, coins or streak.
They live only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them, and there is no backup to restore from.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Only the ads need a connection.
