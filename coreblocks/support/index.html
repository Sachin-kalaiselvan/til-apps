Something broken, or an idea for the game? Email theingredientlist.co@gmail.com and include your device model and Android version — it makes bugs far easier to track down.

Common questions

I lost my high score.
Scores live only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them, and there is no backup to restore from.

The ad didn't give me my reward.
The reward only lands if the ad plays all the way through. If you closed it early, nothing is granted. If it completed and you still got nothing, email us.

Can I turn the sound off?
Yes — the note icon at the top right of the game screen. It remembers your choice.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Only the ads need a connection.
