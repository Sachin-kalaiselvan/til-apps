Privacy Policy

Core Blocks · Last updated 5 October 2026

Core Blocks is published by The Ingredient List, India. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect directly

Nothing. Core Blocks has no account, no login and no server of our own. Your score, your best score and your sound setting are stored only on your own device and are never transmitted to us.

What our advertising partner collects

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google collects your device's Advertising ID along with technical information such as device model, operating system version, coarse location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with ads.

We do not receive this data in an identifiable form. We see only aggregate earnings and performance reports.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children

Core Blocks is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, uninstall it or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → Core Blocks → Storage.

For advertising data, use the Advertising ID controls above, or contact Google directly.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
