Pour Nine

Pour liquid between tubes until each one holds a single colour.

The tubes start mixed. Tap one to pick it up, tap another to pour — the top colour flows across as long as there's room and the colours match. Sort every tube to one colour to clear the level. Every level is checked solvable before you see it, so there is always a way through.

Chapters

  • Clear glassThe base game. Everything is visible.
  • Blind pourOnly the top layer shows. The rest stays hidden until you uncover it.
  • Sealed tubeA spare tube that stays locked until you've made five pours.
  • Full labBlind pours and a sealed tube, together.

Four chapters of 30 levels each.

Daily streak. Coins. Themes you unlock as you play.

Ads are optional: watch a short video for a hint or an undo if you want one. Between-level ads are capped, and never appear in the middle of a level.
