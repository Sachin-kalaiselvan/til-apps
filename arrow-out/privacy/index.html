Privacy Policy

Gridlock Arrows · Last updated 3 November 2026

Gridlock Arrows is published by The Ingredient List, India. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect directly

Nothing. Gridlock Arrows has no account, no login and no server of our own. Your progress, coins, daily streak, unlocked themes and sound setting are stored only on your own device and are never transmitted to us.

What our advertising partner collects

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for a hint or an undo, and a capped number of ads between levels. To serve and measure those ads, Google collects your device's Advertising ID along with technical information such as device model, operating system version, coarse location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with ads.

We do not receive this data in an identifiable form. We see only aggregate earnings and performance reports.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children

Gridlock Arrows is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, uninstall it or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → Gridlock Arrows → Storage.

For advertising data, use the Advertising ID controls above, or contact Google directly.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
