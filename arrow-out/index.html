Arrow Out

Tap an arrow and it slides off the board the way it points. Don't let it hit another.

Every arrow on the board points somewhere. Tap one and it slides off in that direction — but if it runs into another arrow on the way, you lose a heart. Work out the order, clear the board, keep your hearts.

Chapters

  • First lightThe base game. Clear the board without a collision.
  • BlockadeSteel blocks that never move. Arrows have to find a way around them.
  • Double upTwo arrows stacked in one cell. The top one goes first.
  • OverloadBlocks and stacks together, with two hearts instead of three.

Four chapters of 30 levels each, then endless.

Daily streak. Coins. Themes you unlock as you play.

Ads are optional: watch a short video for a hint or an undo if you want one. Between-level ads are capped, and never appear in the middle of a level.
