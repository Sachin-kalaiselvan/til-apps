Arrow Out

Tap an arrow and it flies off the board — if nothing is in its way.

Every arrow on the board points somewhere. Tap one and it slides out in that direction, but only if its path is clear. Work out the order, empty the board, move on to the next.

No timer. Works offline. Free.
