Intervals

Something broken, or an idea for the app? Email theingredientlist.co@gmail.com and include your device model and Android version — it makes bugs far easier to track down.

Common questions

Does it keep running with the screen locked?
Yes. The timer runs as a background service with a notification on your lock screen. If it still stops on your phone, check that battery optimisation isn't restricting Intervals in Android Settings → Apps → Intervals → Battery, and email us your device model.

My music stopped when a cue played.
It should duck (get quieter) and come back, not stop. Some music apps pause on any interruption instead; if yours does, email us which app it is.

I lost my saved timers.
Timers live only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them, and there is no backup to restore from.

Are there ads or a subscription?
No. Intervals has no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Does it work offline?
Yes, completely. It never needs a connection.
