Intervals

Something broken, or an idea for the app? Email theingredientlist.co@gmail.com and include your device model and Android version — it makes bugs far easier to track down.

Common questions

I lost my saved timers.
Presets live only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them, and there is no backup to restore from.

The beep didn't sound.
Check your phone's media volume and that Do Not Disturb isn't silencing the app. If the timer itself stopped when the screen turned off, email us your device model — some phones are aggressive about closing apps in the background.

Are there ads?
No. Intervals has no ads and never will.

Does it work offline?
Yes, completely. It never needs a connection.
