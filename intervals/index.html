Intervals

An interval timer that keeps counting with the screen locked.

Intervals runs as a background service, so it keeps time with your phone locked and in your pocket. Halfway through each interval you get a “Switch” cue, exercise names are spoken aloud, and your music ducks under the cues instead of stopping.

What's in it

  • Presets for Tabata, HIIT, EMOM and Pomodoro
  • Unlimited saved timers
  • Spoken exercise names and a halfway “Switch” cue
  • Music ducks under the cues instead of pausing
  • Lock-screen controls

No ads. No subscription. Works offline. Free.
