Privacy Policy

Intervals · Last updated 3 November 2026

Intervals is published by The Ingredient List, India. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect directly

Nothing. Intervals has no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no server of our own. Your timer presets and settings are stored only on your own device and are never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Ads and third parties

None. Intervals contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics, so no one else receives data from the app either.

Children

Intervals is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, uninstall it or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → Intervals → Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
