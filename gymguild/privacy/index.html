Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026. GymGuild is published by The Ingredient List, Bengaluru.

In short

GymGuild stores some of your data on our server so that your guild can see your training. We don’t show ads, we don’t use analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data. You don’t need an email address or phone number to use the app. It creates an anonymous account for you the first time you open it.

What we store on our server

  • Your display name and city. Other members of your guilds can see them.
  • Your sessions: the activity, when it started, how long it lasted, the distance, any lifts you logged, and how the session was verified. Your guildmates see the activity, the time and the points it earned. They only see your distance if you turn on “Show session details to guildmates” in your profile.
  • Your guilds, kudos, challenges, and any blocks or reports you make.
  • Your gym, if you set one: its name and the spot you saved on the map. We never store a record of where you go.
  • Your bodyweight and ranking standard, if you add them. Only you can see these. We use them to work out your lift ranks.

Our server is run by Supabase in Mumbai, India. Everything sent between the app and the server is encrypted.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos you add to a session.
  • The camera image. During a camera check, the app looks at each frame on your phone to count your reps and then discards it. No video or photo is saved or sent. Only the result is kept: the exercise, the number of reps and how long it took.
  • Your location. During a live session the phone checks whether you are at your saved gym. Only the result, the number of minutes you spent inside, is sent.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location: only if you set a gym, and only while a live session is running.
  • Camera: only when you start a camera check.
  • Notifications: for your live session timer and for alerts about records and challenges.

Who can see your data

Other GymGuild users see only what is listed above, and only inside guilds you belong to. We don’t sell, rent or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile, then Data, then Delete account and data. This permanently removes your account and everything linked to it, from our server and from your phone. You can also email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Age

GymGuild is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page first and let you know in the app before the change takes effect.
