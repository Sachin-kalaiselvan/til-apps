GymGuild privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026. GymGuild is published by The Ingredient List.

The short version

GymGuild needs a small amount of data on our server so your guild can see your training. There are no ads, no analytics and no selling of data. You don’t need an email or a phone number: the app signs you in with an anonymous account tied to this phone.

What is sent to our server

  • Your display name and city, which guildmates can see.
  • Your sessions: activity type, start time, duration, distance, lifts, and how the session was verified. Guildmates see the type, time and credit. Distance is shown only if you turn on Show session details.
  • Your guilds, kudos, challenges, blocks and reports.
  • Your gym: its name and the map position you saved. Your location history is never sent.
  • Your bodyweight and the standards you rank against, if you add them. These are private to you and are used only to work out lift ranks.

The server is run by Supabase in Mumbai, India. Data is sent over an encrypted connection.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos you attach to a session.
  • Camera frames from the camera check. Each frame is compared with the one before it to count reps and thrown away. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Your location during a live session. The phone compares it with your saved gym and sends only the result (minutes inside the gym).

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location, only if you set your gym, and only while a live session is running.
  • Camera, only for the camera check.
  • Notifications, for your live session timer and alerts.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Other GymGuild users see only what is described above, and only inside guilds you belong to.

Deleting your data

Open Profile, then Data, then Delete account and data. This removes your account and everything about you from our server and from the phone. You can also email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

GymGuild is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, this page will be updated first, and the app will tell you.
