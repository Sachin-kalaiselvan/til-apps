Who can use GymGuild

You must be at least 18 years old. Please keep to one account per person.

Be honest about your training

  • Only log sessions you have actually done.
  • Don’t fake sessions, locations, camera checks or lift numbers, and don’t use apps or tools that fake your location or change how GymGuild works.
  • Sessions that look impossible are rejected or removed. If an account cheats repeatedly, we remove it from guilds and leaderboards.

What you can’t post

Other people can see your display name, and the names and descriptions of guilds and challenges you create. These must not include:

  • harassment, bullying, threats or hate towards anyone
  • sexual content or nudity
  • violence, self-harm or dangerous behaviour
  • spam, adverts, scams or links to other services
  • someone else’s personal details, or a name that pretends to be another person
  • anything illegal

Blocking and reporting

You can block or report any member or guild from inside the app. We review reports and may remove content, guilds or accounts that break these terms, without warning.

GymGuild is a training log and a game. It isn’t medical advice. Train within your limits, and talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise programme. You are responsible for your own safety while you exercise.
