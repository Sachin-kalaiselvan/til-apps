GymGuild

Train with your guild. Every sport counts.

Join a guild with your friends or your gym, log any workout, and compete on leaderboards that stay fair. Scores are averaged per member, so a small guild that turns up can beat a big one, and one very active member can’t carry a team.

Verified sessionsCheck in at your gym, or use the camera check: the phone counts your reps and never records any video.
Lifting done properlyLog sets, reps and weight. Get personal records, an estimated one-rep max and a rank for every lift.
Hold your gymEach gym on the map is held by the guild that trained there most this week.
Live session timerYour session sits in the status bar with progress towards your daily goal.

No sign-up form and no ads. Distances and routes stay private unless you choose to share them.
